Why NV

Every piece has a village, a loom and a name behind it

We work with small ateliers and weaving clusters — Banarasi, Kanjivaram, Chanderi, Bandhani, Ajrakh and more — and buy in small batches instead of mass runs. No two dye lots are identical, and that's the point.

  • Direct from the source. We pay weavers before we list the piece, not after it sells.
  • Real fabric notes. Every listing states the weave, dye method and care instructions honestly.
  • Small batches. Limited counts per design, so your piece stays yours.
